# DocTock Support

Welcome to the official support repository for **DocTock** - the document expiry tracker app for iOS.

## About DocTock

Stop letting expired documents catch you off guard.

That moment at the airport when you realize your passport expired last month. The insurance claim denied because your policy lapsed. The fine for driving with an expired license. These moments are stressful, expensive, and completely avoidable.

DocTock keeps track of your important documents so you don't have to. Add your documents once, and we'll make sure you never miss a renewal deadline again.

**Features:**
- Track passports, visas, IDs, insurance, warranties & more
- Smart reminders at 90, 30, and 7 days before expiry
- Schengen 90/180-day visa tracking for Europe travelers
- Privacy-first: we never store sensitive document data

## FAQ

**Q: What data does DocTock store?**
We only store document names and expiry dates. No passport numbers, ID numbers, photos, or sensitive personal information.

**Q: How do I restore my subscription?**
Go to Settings → tap "Restore Purchases".

**Q: Can I use DocTock on multiple devices?**
Yes! Sign in with the same account on any device to sync your documents.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account".
